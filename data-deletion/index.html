資料刪除與撤銷授權說明｜User Data Deletion Instructions

您可以隨時透過以下三種自助管道撤銷 Meta Threads 應用程式授權，或要求完整刪除您於 阿男 GEO 實戰工坊（Anan GEO Workshop） 留存的所有權杖、草稿、圖片素材與歷史貼文紀錄。
You may revoke Meta Threads permissions or request complete deletion of your tokens, drafts, uploaded media, and publishing records from Anan GEO Workshop at any time using any of the three methods below.

METHOD 1・即時自動清除

方式一：於 Threads App 內直接移除應用程式授權｜Method 1: Remove App Directly in Threads

繁體中文操作步驟
  1. 打開手機或網頁版 Threads App，登入您已授權的帳號。
  2. 點選 設定（Settings） → 帳號（Account） → 網站權限（Website permissions）。
  3. 找到本應用程式（Anan Social Relay / 阿男 GEO 實戰工坊），點選 移除（Remove）。
  4. 移除後，Meta 會自動呼叫本系統的加密安全回呼端點，即時清除資料庫中的 threads_user_id 與存取權杖（Access Token），並回傳一組唯一的刪除確認編號（confirmation_code）與查詢狀態網址。
ENGLISH INSTRUCTIONS
  1. Open the Threads app (or web) and sign in to your connected account.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Website permissions.
  3. Select our app (Anan Social Relay / Anan GEO Workshop) and tap Remove.
  4. Meta automatically sends a cryptographically signed callback to our server, which immediately deletes your stored threads_user_id and access token and returns a unique confirmation_code with a public deletion status URL.
資料刪除自動回呼端點（Data Deletion Callback URL） https://relay.anangeo.com/oauth/threads/data-deletion
取消授權自動回呼端點（Deauthorize Callback URL） https://relay.anangeo.com/oauth/threads/deauthorize
刪除狀態查詢端點（Deletion Status Check URL） https://relay.anangeo.com/oauth/threads/deletion-status?code=<confirmation_code>
METHOD 2・LINE 自助解除與完整清除

方式二：於 LINE 聊天室傳送指令或聯繫官方 LINE @250ruauk｜Method 2: Self-Service Command or Official LINE Support

繁體中文操作步驟
  • 即時撤銷 Threads 授權權杖：在您已綁定的 LINE 機器人聊天室中，直接傳送文字指令 解除綁定Threads，系統會立即清除您的 Threads 存取權杖並停止後續同步發文。
  • 申請完整清除草稿與歷史貼文：加入並開啟官方 LINE @250ruauk，傳送「申請刪除全部資料」並附上您的店名或官網網址，我們將為您永久清除店家設定、品牌色票、草稿、圖片素材與歷史貼文紀錄。
ENGLISH INSTRUCTIONS
  • Instant Token Revocation in LINE: Send the message 解除綁定Threads in your connected LINE chat to immediately clear your stored Threads access token and stop automated publishing.
  • Complete Removal of Drafts & History: Message our Official LINE account @250ruauk with your store name or website URL to request full deletion of all drafts, uploaded media assets, brand settings, and historical post logs.

METHOD 3・專責信箱書面刪除

方式三：透過隱私權專責信箱書面申請刪除｜Method 3: Email Request for Complete Data Erasure

繁體中文操作步驟

如果您無法使用 Threads App 或 LINE 帳號，或希望連同官網健檢所留存的電子信箱一併徹底刪除，請直接寄信至專責信箱：

  • 收件信箱：privacy@anangeo.com
  • 信件主旨：資料刪除申請 / Data Deletion Request
  • 信件內容：請提供您的 Threads 用戶名稱（例如 @yourhandle）、店家網址或先前填寫之電子信箱，以便核對身分並精準刪除。
  • 處理時效：核對完成後 48 小時內（最遲 7 個工作天內） 完成清除並以電子郵件回覆確認。
ENGLISH INSTRUCTIONS

If you cannot access Threads or LINE, or wish to erase all stored records including website check report emails, please submit a written request by email:

  • Email Address: privacy@anangeo.com
  • Subject Line: Data Deletion Request / 資料刪除申請
  • Include: Your Threads handle (e.g., @yourhandle), store website URL, or previously submitted email address so we can locate and verify the records.
  • Turnaround Time: Processed and confirmed by email within 48 hours (no later than 7 business days) after verification.
